i What is QRAttendance

QRAttendance records who is in class without calling out names. Students show a time-limited QR code, or tap their school ID on an RFID reader at the door. The professor scans or the reader records it, and the entry is saved with the date and time.

1 Professor: from empty account to attendance

1.1 Set the school year and semester

  1. Sign in. The Overview shows “Set the school year and semester” if no term exists yet.
  2. Enter the school year (for example 2026-2027) and choose the semester.
  3. Press Save term. Everything you create next lives under this term.
  4. To change it later: Workspace settings → add or switch an academic term.

1.2 Choose the primary-teacher attendance mode

Primary/elementary teachers can choose either mode from the dashboard or Classes & sections.

Record by class (subject)

Create the subject first, then add one or more sections under it. These use the regular weekly schedule and a professor-started attendance session.

Record by section

Create the section directly — no subject is needed. In the section’s Attendance windows dialog choose Record only time in, Record only time out, or Record both time in and time out. The enabled morning/afternoon windows each open their own scanner for 30 minutes.

1.3 Create a subject

  1. Go to Classes & sections → Add subject.
  2. Type the course code (for example IT APPDEV 2) and the subject name.
  3. Press Create subject. It appears as a card on the page.

1.4 Add a section with its schedule

  1. Inside the subject, press Add section.
  2. Name the section (for example BSIT 1-A).
  3. The dialog already contains one meeting row. Set the day, start time, end time and room.
  4. Press Add another day for each extra meeting (a lecture on Monday and a lab on Thursday, for example).
  5. Press Create section.
Schedule rules. The same subject may meet on the same day in different sections (Section A in the morning, Section B in the afternoon) — as long as the times do not overlap. One row per day per section is allowed. Saving fails only when a meeting overlaps another section’s meeting, and the message names that section and its time.

1.5 Add students (CSV or by hand)

  1. Open the section, press Import CSV.
  2. The CSV needs three columns: student_id, name, email (a header row is optional, email is optional).
  3. Upload — existing IDs are updated, never duplicated.
  4. Use Add student for one-off changes, the pencil to edit and the bin to remove. Export downloads the current list.

1.6 Invite students to enroll

  1. Press Enrollment QR on the section page (or Invite students by QR on the roster).
  2. Show the QR on your screen, or read out the 16-character section code.
  3. Students scan it, sign in and type their student ID — only IDs already on your list can enroll.

1.7 Take attendance

From the dashboard

On Classes today, press Take attendance — it opens the scanner straight away.

From Live attendance

Live attendance → choose a section → Start session. Sessions stay open 90 minutes.

The camera

Press Start camera and point it at the student’s QR. Each pass lasts 90 seconds and works once.

  1. Students open their page and press Generate my QR.
  2. Your camera reads it and the student is marked present immediately.
  3. Wrong code? The red message tells you if it expired, was already used or belongs to another session.
  4. Press End session when class is over.
Manual corrections. From Attendance history you can open any session and export it; use the section page to keep the roster accurate.

1.8 History and export

  • Attendance history groups sessions by school year and semester.
  • Each session shows attendance, its rate, and a QR / RFID badge for how students checked in.
  • Export downloads a CSV that opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

1.9 Workspace settings

  • Academic terms — add, switch or delete a term. Changing the current term changes what you see on Classes, Roster and History.
  • Your account — edit your name, email, school level and profile picture in My profile.
  • This manual — view, download or print it from this page.

2 Student: joining and checking in

  1. Create an account on the sign-up page: pick Student, enter Last Name, First Name, Middle Name and your student ID.
  2. Join a class — scan your professor’s enrollment QR, or type the 16-character code on the Join a class page.
  3. Your student ID must match the professor’s class list. If it doesn’t, ask your professor to check it.
  4. Check in by QR — when attendance is open you’ll see Generate my QR. Show it to the professor’s camera.
  5. Check in by card — tap your linked school ID on the classroom reader. One short beep and a green light means you’re recorded.
  6. My attendance lists every check-in with the date, time and status.
Keep your ID current. The QR pass expires after 90 seconds — refresh it if class is running late.

3 RFID readers and school ID cards

Pair a reader (professor)

RFID readers → Add reader → choose a section. You get a 6-character code that lasts 5 minutes. Press Pair on the reader, join its Wi-Fi portal and type the code.

Link a card (student)

Link school ID → Link my school ID → you get a 6-character code. Press Pair on the reader, type the code, then tap your card. One card belongs to one student.

Watch it live

The reader page and the scanner page show online status, the last student who tapped and how many taps happened today.

4 Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
“Incorrect email or password”Check the email is the one you signed up with. There is no reset link yet — ask your administrator.
Student can’t enrollTheir student ID must already be on the section roster (import the CSV first).
QR says “expired”Passes last 90 seconds. Generate a new one and scan it quickly.
“Already used”That pass was scanned once already — that’s the security working.
Schedule error when savingAnother section already meets on that day and time. Pick a different day/time.
Reader shows offlineWi-Fi or server unreachable. The reader retries every 20 seconds; check its screen and your site URL.
Camera won’t startHTTPS is required for camera access. Allow camera permission, then press Start camera again.
Student picture not visibleUploads need the uploads/avatars folder to be writable by PHP.
